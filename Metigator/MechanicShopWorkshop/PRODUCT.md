# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mechanic shop owners, admins, and employees use this system during daily workshop operations. Admins need clear control over customers, employees, vehicles, work orders, schedules, invoices, and parts. Employees need a practical interface for seeing assigned work, understanding the next task, and updating operational progress without friction.

## Product Purpose

MechanicShop is a workshop management system that organizes the core flow of a mechanic business: customer intake, vehicle records, work orders, labor assignment, repair tasks, scheduling, parts, billing, and invoicing. Success means the interface feels reliable, efficient, and professional enough to demo as a real junior developer portfolio project.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, practical.

The product should feel like a serious operational tool with a polished, high-quality finish. It should combine clean professional structure, modern product UI patterns, and a subtle garage-tech character without becoming flashy or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

Avoid a cheap default Bootstrap look, childish styling, excessive colors, overly dark screens, noisy gradients, too much animation, complicated layouts, and decorative effects that do not help the workflow. The interface should not feel like a marketing landing page or an AI-generated card grid.

## Design Principles

1. Prioritize the workflow over decoration.
2. Make daily workshop actions fast to scan and easy to repeat.
3. Use premium restraint: polished spacing, typography, and state design before visual effects.
4. Keep admin and employee experiences consistent while respecting their different responsibilities.
5. Make every important screen demo-ready for job interviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target clear readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, obvious focus states, and responsive layouts that work on laptop and tablet-sized screens. Motion should be subtle, functional, and safe for reduced-motion users.
